# content/objectives

Neutral epic objective definitions — the elemental Wyrms (`emberwyrm`,
`frostwyrm`, `stormwyrm`, `stonewyrm`) and the Elder `aeonwyrm` that rotate through
the Wyrm Pit, plus the north-river epics `rift_vanguard` (early) and `the_sunderer`
(late). Pit rotation/timing lives in `wyrm-pit.ron` and `sunder-hollow.ron`; the
buffs, drops and river reward are in `objective-rewards.ron`. All names are original
IP (Law 5) — not Riot's. Placements are in `../maps/riftline-crucible.ron`.

Closed vocabulary lives in `content/_schema/vocabulary.ron` (copied canonical from
the moon-lol fork/original-ip). All `.ron` here is CI-gated by `tools/content-lint`
against `_schema`.
