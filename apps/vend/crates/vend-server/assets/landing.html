Built for founders OpenRouter does not serve

Your brand, not theirs

Your users never see OpenRouter. The endpoint, the invoice and the dashboard are yours.

Non-US top-ups

Mint checkouts cover iyzico and USDC alongside Stripe, Creem, Polar and PayPal — markets Stripe alone does not reach.

Per-user wallets

Every user gets a prepaid balance with its own daily spend cap, enforced atomically — no shared pool to drain.

Success-only billing

A hold is placed first; only the exact provider cost plus your markup is ever captured. Failures release automatically.

Automatic payouts

Every charge splits into provider cost, your margin and the platform fee on Mint — idempotent, with a reconciler for interrupted legs.

One API, every model

The full OpenAI-compatible surface, streaming included, across whatever Relay routes — no separate integration per provider.

Pricing

What you pay Vend
Platform feea share of your margin on captured charges, set by the operator at launch — not a per-seat or per-request fee
Provider costpassed through exactly; Vend adds no markup of its own on top of what you charge your users
Hostingrun it yourself (open path) or on the operator's infrastructure

Compare that to reselling through a provider's own billing: you do not control the price, the invoice, or when you get paid. On Vend you set the markup and Mint pays you out.
