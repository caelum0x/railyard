Generators: TRELLIS firtoz/trellis · Hunyuan3D-2.1 ndreca/hunyuan3d-2.1 (PBR texture + auto-rig) · Hunyuan3D-2mv tencent/hunyuan3d-2mv (single front view).
Verdict — winner: Hunyuan3D-2.1
Hunyuan3D-2.1 is clearly the best stylized game-ready result on both champions: sharpest geometry, faithful PBR texture, and it preserves flowing/ornate detail the baseline loses (e.g. Zeus's full flowing cape and layered gold armor; Anubis's crisp collar, kilt glyphs and fluffy tail). ~$0.27/run, ~110–125s. Minor artifacts to QA: occasional glitch shard where the concept holds a prop (Zeus's lightning bolt) and slight face-texture bleed.
TRELLIS (baseline) is acceptable and cheap but noticeably softer/“melted” — blobby hands, truncated Zeus cape, less crisp ornamentation.
Hunyuan3D-2mv produced clean geometry but no texture (white clay) from a single front image — not usable as-is without true multi-view inputs or a separate texture pass, despite being cheapest/fastest ($0.11, ~12–17s).
Recommendation: switch the full 47-batch to Hunyuan3D-2.1 (~47 × $0.27 ≈ $12.70 total), with a light artifact-QA pass. Keep TRELLIS only as a cheap fallback; skip 2mv unless fed multi-view refs.
